CallRec

Record Xiaomi calls straight from the browser over USB. No installed app, no Shizuku — it runs a small helper on the phone through ADB.

Status: not connected
  1. On the phone: Developer options → turn on USB debugging and USB debugging (Security settings).
  2. Plug in by USB, click Connect, and accept the "Allow USB debugging" prompt.

After "Start watching" the phone records every call on its own — you can unplug it. Recordings are kept until reboot or Stop. "Load recordings" plays and downloads them here.

With the REC button on, a floating button sits on the screen for the length of each call: tap it to pause or resume the recording, drag it out of the way (it remembers where). Untick "Record every call automatically" to record only the calls where you tap it. Change these, then Stop and Start watching again.
